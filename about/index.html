I am currently pursuing my PhD at the National University of Singapore, advised by Abhik Roychoudhury. For my research, I am exploring automated testing, program analysis, and agentic approaches to uncover security vulnerabilities in software at scale.

Before starting my PhD, I completed my master’s at NUS under Manuel Rigger. Prior to that, I worked as a Product Security Engineer with Tarah Wheeler at Red Queen Dynamics, following several years of developing Python software and contributing to open source through Google Summer of Code as both a student and mentor.

Outside of research, I occasionally do bug bounty hunting and have recently gotten into photography.
